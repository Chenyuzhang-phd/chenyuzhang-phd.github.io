Developing a Professional Learning Module for Generative AI-Supported Written Feedback — Chinese Higher Education Context

Generative AI tools (e.g., ChatGPT) are increasingly used in higher education to provide written feedback, but their effectiveness largely depends on teachers' ability to use these tools critically. Currently, most teachers lack systematic guidance and struggle to effectively integrate AI tools into instructional assessment. This study aims to develop and evaluate a professional learning module for generative AI-supported written feedback for Chinese university teachers, bridging the gap between rapid technology adoption and sound pedagogical integration.

Generative AI Written Feedback Teacher Professional Learning Design & Development Research Higher Education
1

Needs Analysis Phase

Through focus group interviews (16-24 frontline university teachers) and literature analysis, understand the difficulties teachers face when using generative AI for written feedback and the gaps in existing teacher professional learning resources.

RQ1: What difficulties do teachers encounter when using AI for written feedback?
RQ2: What gaps exist in current teacher professional learning resources in addressing these difficulties?
2

Design & Development Phase

Based on needs analysis and literature review, develop the professional learning module and invite 6 domain experts for structured review. Ensure content validity through I-CVI index (≥0.78), forming evidence-based design principles.

RQ3: What design principles does the module embody?
RQ4: How do experts evaluate the appropriateness of the module design?
3

Implementation & Evaluation Phase

Recruit 15-20 university teachers to participate in an 8-week module implementation (4 structured sessions, biweekly). Evaluate actual effectiveness through feedback sample scoring, reflective journals, semi-structured interviews, and usability questionnaires.

RQ5: How do participating teachers implement the module's design principles?
RQ6: How do teachers evaluate the usability of the module?
Research Methodology

Employing the Design and Development Research (DDR) methodology, systematically developing educational intervention products through four phases: analysis, design, development, and evaluation. Unlike the iterative cycles of Design-Based Research (DBR), DDR aims to produce usable products with clear design principles, typically involving only one round of internal revision.

Expected Outcomes

Design Principles Set: Evidence- and theory-based design principles for generative AI-supported written feedback professional learning, providing a framework for core pedagogical, technical, and ethical considerations.

Professional Learning Module: A fully functional learning module that operationalizes design principles, empowering university teachers to use generative AI for written feedback effectively and responsibly.
